The Sanctuary Standard

A campaign to establish legally recognised, low-demand, sensory-safe environments in public and civic infrastructure. Replacing mandatory masking with physical sovereignty across all neurodivergent profiles.

CAMPAIGN STATUS ACTIVE ADVOCACY
TARGET JURISDICTION PUBLIC & CIVIC SPACES
CORE DEMAND ZERO MASKING TAX

THE THREE PILLARS OF SANCTUARY

01

Acoustic & Visual Regulation

Mandatory low-sensory zoning within civic facilities, including designated quiet corridors, flicker-free lighting protocols, and total elimination of high-decibel ambient audio pollution in essential public services.

02

Literal & Direct Communication

Enforcement of zero-subtext signage and literal assistance options. Public infrastructure must provide clear, predictable, multi-modal navigation that removes the cognitive penalty of ambiguous directions.

03

Low-Demand Infrastructure

Establishing non-verbal service channels, zero-eye-contact interaction options, and physical retreat spaces designed to prevent sensory overload and autonomic nervous system burnout.

SYSTEM INTEGRATED ACCESS

Nimbus Access Card

Dynamic Venue Adjustments & Online Booking

The Access Card translates your specific disability and accessibility requirements into recognised universal symbols. It links directly with major UK ticketing platforms, allowing you to secure adjustments automatically without awkward phone calls or repetitive explanations.

◆
Essential Companion (+1 Symbol): Indicates you need additional support to participate and may enable a ticket concession at venues, theatres, and events.
◆
Queue Assistance (Crowds or Standing Symbols): Allows access to fast-track or quiet queueing for environments where long standing triggers sensory overload or fatigue.
◆
Direct System Integration: Links directly to online ticketers like AXS and major theatre box offices for automatic booking adjustments.

Forged Out of Daily Friction

Navigating the world as a confirmed AuDHD individual (Autism Spectrum Disorder & ADHD Combined Presentation) means living at a constant neurological intersection: balancing the autistic need for predictability, quiet regulation, and zero-subtext clarity against the kinetic energy, sensory seeking, and rapid context-switching of ADHD.

Forcing dual neurodivergent processing into rigid neurotypical environments creates a massive, silent "Masking Tax": a continuous drain on executive function, working memory, and nervous system regulation just to navigate daily life.

THE WORKBENCH COMMITMENT

"Instead of spending a lifetime apologising for an operating system the world wasn't built for, I started mapping it. Every Neuro-Code™ in this library and every physical deck cut at my workbench comes directly from lived experience."

I build this ecosystem as a solo architect because accessibility shouldn't be hidden behind paywalls, diluted by corporate training agencies, or locked away behind clinical jargon.

While AuDHMe™ was born out of my own authentic AuDHD lived experience, the physical tools, tactile apparel, and zero-subtext card protocols we build are engineered to support anyone navigating sensory overload, executive dysfunction, or communication friction across the entire neurodivergent community.

The digital framework will always remain open-access and free for grassroots community self-advocacy. When you back this project or order hardware, you are funding raw industrial materials directly on my workbench to turn these digital codes into physical tools for real-world independence.
