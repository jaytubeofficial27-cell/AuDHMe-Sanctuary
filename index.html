AuDHMe™ Sanctuary Hub

We aren't broken neurotypicals, we're running a completely different operating system. AuDHMe is built from real lived experience to eliminate the "Masking Tax", translate executive dysfunction, executive age, and sensory overload into plain English, and put actual physical tools in your hands when your words run out.

Challenging workplace and public norms at the root. We're pushing for legally recognised sensory-safe rooms, zero-subtext signage, and low-demand public zones so nobody has to burn out their nervous system just to earn a pay cheque.
