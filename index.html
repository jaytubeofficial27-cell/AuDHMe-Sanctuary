    CIVIC LEGISLATION & INFRASTRUCTURE DIRECTIVE

    The Sanctuary Standard

    A campaign to establish legally recognised, low-demand, sensory-safe rooms and protocols in public and civic buildings, so neurodivergent people aren't forced to pay the Masking Tax just to access everyday life.

    CAMPAIGN STATUS ACTIVE ADVOCACY
    TARGET UK PUBLIC & CIVIC SPACES
    CORE GOAL MANDATORY SENSORY ROOMS
    PILLAR 01

    Acoustic & Visual Regulation

    Mandatory low-sensory rooms and quiet zones inside public buildings, flicker-free lighting, and cutting out relentless background music and announcements in essential services.

    PILLAR 02

    Literal, Zero-Subtext Signage

    Clear, literal wayfinding and direct assistance options so navigating a hospital, station, or council building doesn't burn through your entire executive function battery before you even arrive.

    PILLAR 03

    Non-Verbal & Low-Demand Access

    Written/text service channels, zero-eye-contact penalties, and physical retreat spaces to prevent sensory overload from turning into a public meltdown or shutdown.

    Living at the AuDHD Intersection

    Living as a confirmed AuDHD person means your brain is in a constant tug-of-war with itself. My autistic side needs routine, quiet predictability, and literal, zero-subtext honesty. My ADHD side craves novelty, kinetic stimulation, and runs on an interest-based nervous system where mundane admin feels physically impossible until the last-minute panic kicks in.

    When you try to force that dual operating system into a neurotypical world, while dealing with executive dysfunction, the gap between your chronological age and your executive age, sensory overload, and time blindness, you pay a massive, invisible Masking Tax every single day.

    WHY I STARTED MAPPING THIS

    "Instead of spending the rest of my life apologising for having an operating system the world wasn't built for, I started writing the manual for it. Every Neuro-Code in this library and every card cut on my workbench comes straight from real life."

    I started AuDHMe as a solo founder because our community doesn't need more patronising corporate pamphlets or paywalled clinical jargon. Whether you're autistic, ADHD, AuDHD, dyslexic, dyspraxic, OCD, or navigating sensory and processing differences, this space and the tools we build are here so you can advocate for yourself without having to apologise for how your brain works.
